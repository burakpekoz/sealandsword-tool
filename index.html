Seal & Sword / Mühür ve Kılıç — Publishing Tool

History documentaries about decisions that changed history, in English (Seal & Sword, @sealandsword) and Turkish (Mühür ve Kılıç, @muhurvekilic).

This site describes the private publishing tool the channel owner uses to manage these two channels. The tool:

  • uploads the channel owner's own finished videos, thumbnails and captions to these two channels,
  • schedules publish times and sets titles and descriptions, and posts the owner's own comments,
  • reads the channels' own YouTube Analytics to plan future episodes.

It is used only by the channel owner. It is not a public service, and it does not access any other user's data.
