Privacy Policy — Seal & Sword / Mühür ve Kılıç Publishing Tool

Last updated: 7 November 2026

This tool is a private, internal publishing tool. It is used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Seal & Sword (@sealandsword) and Mühür ve Kılıç (@muhurvekilic) to manage those two channels. It is not offered to the public and has no other users.

What the tool does

  • Uploads videos, thumbnails and captions that the channel owner produced to the owner's own two channels.
  • Sets titles, descriptions, tags, publish times on those videos, and posts the owner's own comments and replies on them.
  • Reads the channels' own statistics (YouTube Analytics) to plan future videos.

What data it accesses

The tool only accesses data of the owner's own YouTube channels, through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, after the owner signs in with Google OAuth and gives consent. It does not access, collect or store data about any other YouTube user. Public comments may be read only on the owner's own videos, in order to reply to them.

Storage and sharing

  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the owner's personal computer and are never shared.
  • Channel statistics are kept only on that computer for planning purposes.
  • No data is sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

Revoking access

The owner can revoke the tool's access at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.

The tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you are also subject to the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Deletion of data

When access is revoked, or at the owner's request, the stored authorization tokens and saved channel statistics are deleted from the owner's computer within 7 days. Saved channel statistics reports are automatically deleted after 30 days.
